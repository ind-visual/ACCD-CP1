Pleiades (Seven Sisters)

Among the most widely recognised star clusters in human history, the Pleiades appears in the cosmologies of Aboriginal Australian, Māori, West African, Andean, Islamic, Greek, and Japanese traditions, and many others beyond these. Each culture looked up and found something different: sisters, a canoe, a harvest sign, a gateway. The same sky, read through entirely different ways of knowing. This map places the traditions that shaped this experience inside that same shared light.
