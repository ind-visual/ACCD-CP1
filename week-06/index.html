Project Overview

This page documents a 3D prototype for the Nest Thermostat Energy Dashboard using Blender. The process includes initial sketches, digital renders, motion studies, and final high-fidelity compositions. Each stage was documented to show the evolution of the concept to balance form, function, and final prototype renders.
